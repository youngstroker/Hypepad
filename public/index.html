How it works

From SOL to a live market

Four steps, all on-chain and visible. If anything fails after you pay, your SOL is refunded automatically.

  1. SOL HYPE
    01

    Pay in SOL

    Fill in your coin and approve one transfer in your Solana wallet. The quote shows every fee before you sign.

  2. 02

    Deployed on HyperEVM

    Once your payment is final on Solana (about 15 seconds), the coin is created with your initial buy in the same transaction.

  3. 03

    Trade on the curve

    Buy with SOL here, or buy and sell with HYPE from any HyperEVM wallet. The price follows a public formula.

  4. Graduated · LP burned
    04

    Graduate to HyperSwap

    When the curve sells out, the raised HYPE and the remaining 20% seed a HyperSwap pool. The LP tokens are burned, so that liquidity stays.
